# Digital health on one page

One figure of the US digital health landscape, built for the Stanford Biodesign for Digital Health course: the same six stakeholders (patients and families, pharma and medtech, digital health companies, providers, health insurance, and schools, CBOs and public agencies) shown three ways — who makes the rules, how data moves, and how money moves.

**Live page:** https://stanfordbdhg.github.io/DHOnePage/

- Hover over (or tap) any dotted acronym on the page to see it spelled out and defined; the definitions come from the glossary.
- `glossary.md` is the companion glossary used in the course (acronym, spelled-out form, one-sentence definition); `glossary.html` is the same content as a web page, linked from the figure.
- The page is a single self-contained `index.html` (inline styles, no build step) plus the logo in `assets/`. It scales to fit the browser window; printing or "Save as PDF" produces a single 2440 × 1620 px page.

Learn more about the course: https://med.stanford.edu/biodesign/programs/stanford-courses/biodesign-for-digital-health.html

Questions, comments, or feedback: aydinz@stanford.edu. Many thanks to Meghana Nerurkar for inspiring the creation of this diagram.

Payment model categories follow the framework in Zahedivash et al., *npj Digital Medicine* 2026.
