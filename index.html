Overarching Legislation: FD&C Act (1938) what counts as a drug or device · HIPAA (1996) privacy baseline · HITECH (2009) $30B+ to adopt EHRs · ACA (2010) Medicaid expansion, marketplaces, CMMI, ACOs, Sunshine Act · MACRA (2015) quality-based Medicare pay · 21st Century Cures (2016) information blocking, APIs, CDS carve-out, RWE framework. Most of the agencies below sit inside HHS; the rest are the FTC, DEA, Education, the courts and the states.
FTC · data handling rules for consumer apps that fall outside of HIPAA: deceptive claims (UDAP), breach notice (HBNR), COPPA for under-13s
FDA · safety, efficacy, marketing rules. CDRH (devices, including SaMD): is it a device? Intended use and claims decide, not the hardware. General wellness → not regulated. CDS → exempt only if the clinician can check the basis. SaMD → Class I / II / III, cleared via 510(k), De Novo or PMA; Breakthrough Devices speeds review; AI devices need GMLP and a PCCP; connected devices, cybersecurity (524B). CDER / CBER (drugs, biologics): IND → trials → NDA / BLA; a drug packaged with an app is a combination product; DTC drug ads are policed by OPDP. Investigational use of anything needs an IND or IDE plus an IRB. RWD/RWE from devices and apps increasingly count as evidence. TEMPO: use inside CMS's ACCESS Model before clearance.
OCR · HIPAA for covered entities (providers, plans, clearinghouses) and their business associates (BAAs): all BA's (like digital health companies) need to handle data according to HIPAA requirements and are liable for information breaches · ACA Section 1557: no discriminatory algorithms or decision tools
Department of Education · FERPA governs student records, including a lot of school health data, instead of HIPAA
States & AGs · privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA, CMIA, MHMDA); AI laws (CA, CO, IL, NY); Attorneys General investigate and sue
ONC / ASTP · certifies EHRs (CEHRT: USCDI, FHIR APIs), enforces information blocking rules to allow data exchange, runs TEFCA
CMS · Medicare: PFS codes & rates, coverage (NCD/LCD), MA Star Ratings · Medicaid: 1115 waivers, ILOS, MCO rules · CCIIO: ACA plans
Public health & local government · CDC and state agencies run registries (IIS, PDMP) and surveillance; local budgets fund school health
Courts · product liability and malpractice apply with no new law needed (see the Character.AI and OpenAI suits)
OIG / DOJ · AKS ('free' software needs a safe harbor), Stark, FCA · Sunshine Act: payments to doctors go public
Buyer gates · not law, but required to sell: SOC 2 / HITRUST, a signed BAA, EHR integration, an IRB for any pilot with patients
States · licensure & scope of practice (only humans), CPOM → MSO + PC, IMLC · DEA controlled substances Rx · SAMHSA 42 CFR Part 2
CMMI · tests new payment models: ACCESS (tech-enabled chronic care; FDA's TEMPO rides along), ASPIRE & InCK (Medicaid kids), ACOs
EU · GDPR, the MDR and the AI Act apply the moment you have a European user
Patient & Family
Generates the data: wearables, apps, portal visits. Pays premiums, taxes, copays and sometimes a D2C subscription. About half of US kids are on Medicaid or CHIP.
Pharma & MedTech
Drug, biologic and device makers. To the DH company, they are a customer, a data provider, or a distribution channel (the app ships with the device/pharma product). Sometimes the Pharma or MedTech company is the DH company.
Digital Health Company
App, device, SaMD, DTx, SaaS. Or, a pharma or device company's digital arm. Sells D2C, by subscription (SaaS, PMPM), or bills insurance itself, which requires licensed clinicians (MSO + PC). HIPAA applies to DH company through a BAA.
Providers: Clinicians & Health Systems
Generally, only licensed humans can deliver care. The EHR records it in SNOMED CT, LOINC, RxNorm; bills it in ICD-10-CM + CPT/HCPCS. Share records via HIE, TEFCA/QHINs; report to IIS, PDMP.
Health Insurance
Medicare (Parts A, B, C = MA, D). Medicaid & CHIP, mostly via MCOs (EPSDT guarantees kids' benefits). Commercial & employer plans (ESI, self-insured via a TPA), PBMs. Funded by premiums and taxes; CMS sets the fee schedule.
Schools, CBOs & Public Agencies
The other sector, especially for kids: school-based health (bills Medicaid), CBOs, local programs, public health. School records: covered under FERPA, not HIPAA. Pays for prevention whose savings aren't seen in the health sector.
Value-based care depends on quality metrics: HEDIS (NCQA) · Star Ratings (MA) · Child & Adult Core Sets (Medicaid/CHIP) · MIPS/QPP & Promoting Interoperability · eCQMs · CAHPS · HCC risk adjustment · SDOH/HRSN screening
